I've updated the [JUnit AntiPatterns](/junit/anti-patterns/) article after receiving a number of emails giving feedback on suggested improvements and new anti-patterns. I've made the following changes:

- Added sections for [Misuse of Assertions](/junit/antipatterns.html#Misuse_of_Assertions) and [Redundant Assertions](/junit/antipatterns.html#Redundant_Assertions), and reorganised the anti-patterns that relate to assertions.
- Anti-pattern examples and the refactored solutions are now styled differently.
