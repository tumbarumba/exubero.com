Yes, it's Yet Another JUnit Antipatterns Update. This time, I've tried to apply all the feedback I received from the find folks on the [JUnit mailing list](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/junit/). Specifically, I posted a [notice](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/junit/message/16150) about my last update on the list, and received an absolute flurry of responses from unit testing luminaries near and far.

As a result of all this, the [JUnit Anti-patterns](/junit/antipatterns.html) page has been overhauled yet again. The major changes are:

- Added section on using the wrong assert
- Expanded on the unexpected exceptions section
- Expanded on the multiple asserts section
- Avoided using the title "Refactorings"
- Linked to an explicit license
