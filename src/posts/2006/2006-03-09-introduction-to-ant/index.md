Last year I put together a few training slides to allow me to present a course on [Ant](http://ant.apache.org/) to a group of people and other assorted creatures. I've finally got around to putting these notes online as a new resource on Exubero: [Introduction to Ant](/ant/antintro-s5.html). Hopefully, these will be useful to anyone trying to learn about Ant, and a great head start for more experienced users of Ant who need to teach others.

If anyone has suggestions for improvements or corrections regarding the slides, I'd like to hear back from you.
