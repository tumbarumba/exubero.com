The [JUnit mailing list](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/junit/) is the place to go when asking about questions not on the [FAQ](http://junit.sourceforge.net/doc/faq/faq.htm "Frequently Asked Questions"). However, being a moderated list, it can sometimes take a long time for the emails to work their way through the moderation queue. I've been stung a few times myself; an email I sent once took 5 days to finally get posted. This makes it very hard to hold an intelligent conversation via the list, as continuity gets broken.

To help fix this situation, J. B. Rainsberger [asked for help](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/junit/message/16714). Remembering my previous problems, I volunteered, and my offer was [accepted](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/junit/message/16739). It's nice to be able to help out in something I believe in, and I'm sure that when I mention my special responsibilities at dinner parties, people will be awed and amazed.
