This is a recurring problem I have: trying to explain my aversion to the [Singleton Pattern](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singleton_pattern) (hereby re-christened the **Singleton Anti-pattern**). I've finally tracked down a number of links that explain the issue. There have been a few articles written over the years, but these are my favourites:

- [Singleton Considered Stupid](http://steve.yegge.googlepages.com/singleton-considered-stupid)
- [Use your Singletons Wisely](http://www-128.ibm.com/developerworks/webservices/library/co-single.html)
- [Why Singletons are Evil](http://blogs.msdn.com/scottdensmore/archive/2004/05/25/140827.aspx)
