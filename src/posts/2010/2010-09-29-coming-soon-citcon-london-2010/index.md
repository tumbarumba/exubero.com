The European version of [CITCON](http://www.citconf.com/) (the Continuous Integration and Testing Conference) had a last minute change of plan, and has switched locations to [London](http://www.citconf.com/london2010/)! This is one of my favourite conferences (see my notes on [2006](/2006/08/17/citcon-london-2006/) and [2009](/2009/09/19/citcon-europe-2009/)), and I'm really happy to see it coming back to London.

Registrations have only been open for a week, but more than 100 of the 150 places have been filled. Here's a message to anyone who can make it to London for the 5th and 6th November: [register](http://www.citconf.com/london2010/register.php) now... and see you there :-)
