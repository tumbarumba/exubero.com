On a whim, I joined some of my work colleagues going to XTC. Tonight was a special event: **Not Agile 2010**. For those of us who didn't/couldn't/wouldn't go to the [Agile 2010](http://agile2010.agilealliance.org/) conference in Florida, we had an evening of lighting talks from anyone able stand upright and speak anything that sounded vaguely like English. With that basic criteria in place, there were a number of pretty interesting talks. It was also the first time I actually used my twitter account after signing up 2 years ago, giving a very different view of the talks from the [#NotAgile2010](http://twitter.com/#search?q=%23NotAgile2010) tag. I posted a number of pretty banal observations from my Tumbarumba id on Twitter, but there was a lot of other interesting details I picked up from that tag.
