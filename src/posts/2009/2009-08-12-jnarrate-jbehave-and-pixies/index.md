I found the [XTC meetup](http://www.meetup.com/Extreme-Tuesday-Club/calendar/11040375/) to be a fascinating event. There were a few familiar faces I remembered from previous years, and quite a few new ones. There was a palpable buzz in the room, which got quite crowded as more people turned up.

I had a long chat with a guy who develops the website for the Tate (hi Bob!). For me, the most interesting part of the night was an introduction to [Behaviour Driven Development](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavior_Driven_Development) (BDD), and the tools that support the paradigm. I was vaguely aware of the term, but I hadn't realised how mature the movement had become. [Liz Keogh](http://lizkeogh.com/) talked at length regarding [feature injection](http://lizkeogh.com/2008/09/10/feature-injection-and-handling-technical-stories/) (and curiously, pixies). [Antony Marcano](http://antonymarcano.com) demonstrated [JNarrate](http://jnarrate.org), which allows behaviour specifications to be written in an amazing literate type of Java code. Very eye opening stuff, and I regret not turning up to the meetings for a long time.
