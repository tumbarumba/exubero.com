I liked Jason Yip's posting: [No matter how many times you say it, we still don't need a QA on the team](http://jchyip.blogspot.com/2009/01/no-matter-how-many-times-you-say-it-we.html) - it really pins down the definition of the QA/QC terms.

In summary:

**Quality Assurance (QA):** The design and implementation of processes (roles + activities) to _ensure quality_ in a system/product/service.

**Quality Control (QC):** The design and implementation of processes (roles + activities) to _verify the quality_ of a system/product/service.

QA = prevent defects; QC = detect defects.
