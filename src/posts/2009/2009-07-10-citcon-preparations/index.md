I've just booked my hotel and Eurostar tickets to [CITCON Paris 2009](http://citconf.com/paris2009/). I'm quite excited to be going back to Paris - it's actually been 8 years since I was last there. My complete ignorance of the French language will make it all the more fun, I'm sure.

I had a look at the [list of registrants](http://citconf.com/wiki/index.php?title=CITCONEurope2009Registrants), and I was quite pleased to see that there are quite a few people I've met before on various jaunts around London and elsewhere. I won't be completely in an unknown group. Let's hope it will be as much fun as [my last CITCON](/2006/10/12/citcon-london-2006-retrospective/).
