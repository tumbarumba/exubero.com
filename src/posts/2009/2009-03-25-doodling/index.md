In case you missed it, doodling is not a bad thing any more:

- [The Guardian](http://www.guardian.co.uk/science/2009/feb/27/doodling-doodles-boring-meetings-concentration)
- [BBC](http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/health/7912671.stm)

![Doodle of my thumb](/2009/03/25/doodling/images/doodle-thumb.png "Doodle of my thumb")

This is lucky for me. As anyone who has sat next to me in a meeting can testify, my notebooks are filled with dubious pen scribblings and "artwork".

![Small doodle](/2009/03/25/doodling/images/doodle-small.png "Small doodle")

My left hand features prominently in my notebook, mainly because it happens to be there right in front of me. Also seen are pens, cups, books, computers, and other pieces of meeting room paraphernalia. Not much faces, though. I can never get those looking right.
