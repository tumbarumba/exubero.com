I guess John (the hairy guy with the beard) and I (the bald guy with the goatee) must have made some impression. I must admit, it is amazingly accurate! Or at least it will be, when I start wearing bright orange shirts and dying my beard blue. Otherwise, I probably _was_ sitting back with a cup of tea, daydreaming of something unrelated (or more likely, [doodling](/2009/03/25/doodling/)), as shown in the picture.
