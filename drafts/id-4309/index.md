Jeff's goal:

- reduce suffering in software development (or globally)

Andy's goal (interim):

- create a living software body of knowledge
    - "living":
        - people have to be breathing life into it
        - show up and discuss issue
        - Not SWEBOK because: it's dead
    - I know I have succeeded when...
        - I know there is a software development field
        - as opposed to independant people re-inventing the wheel

Joe's goal:

- Make UBS a place that I would like to recruit others into
    - I know I have succeeded when...
        - Good people, I know, stay workng there (stop the bleeding)
        - Others around me are active in the internal development community
        - I don't have the sense that those around me are just coming in, doing their work, and going home. They are engaged
        - The extra engagement happens during the work hours
        - The developers have control over the process that they execute
        - Developers are seen as a separate, and highly skilled, discipline
        - Developers have frequent communication with the people who care about the outcomes
    - I know I've failed when...
        - People are asked to do things without having a clear understanding of why
        - The rules imposed by the outside are used in draconian ways
        - Internal rules are impossible to change and used an excuse not to change
